# Features

## Key Features

- **Analytics Dashboard** – A single screen covering sales & revenue, orders & checkout, products & catalog, customers & retention, and returns — no manual report navigation needed.
- **AI Analytics Workspace** – Conversational AI interface that answers plain-English questions about revenue, orders, products, categories, customers, discounts, carts, returns, and regions using approved nopCommerce store data.
- **Saved Prompts** – Save recurring reports and commonly used business questions so your team can re-run them in one click, week after week.
- **Prompt Activity Log** – Track every AI analytics request, its status (e.g. Success, Needs Clarification), and when it ran.
- **Excel Export** – Export dashboard data to `.xlsx`.
- **CSV / PDF Export** – Export AI Analytics Workspace answers to CSV or PDF for sharing with finance, operations, or management.
- **Multi-Provider AI Support** – Connect to OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude), OpenRouter (Gemini, LLaMA, Mistral, and others), Azure OpenAI, or any custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
- **Date Range & Store Filtering** – Filter all dashboard data by custom date range, quick-range shortcuts, and by individual store in multi-store setups.
- **License Registration** – Register and view license status directly from the Configuration screen.

## Analytics Dashboard Reports

### Sales & Revenue
- Revenue summary and revenue trend
- Monthly and annual sales trends
- Average order value (AOV) and average items per order
- Sales by region (country/state from billing or shipping address)
- Payment method performance breakdown
- Shipping method performance breakdown

### Orders & Checkout
- Total orders and order status breakdown (Pending, Processing, Complete, Cancelled)
- Orders from new vs. returning customers
- Discount usage reports
- Pending orders and cart items updated (cart abandonment signal)

### Products & Catalog
- Top-selling products (quantity and revenue, ranked)
- Popular products over time
- Category-wise performance (quantity and revenue, ranked)

### Customers & Retention
- User registrations trend (daily/weekly/monthly/yearly views)
- Active customers
- Average Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Customer retention rate (e.g. % of customers making a second purchase within 90 days)
- Customer churn trend
- Customer segmentation (order value, purchase frequency, region, guest vs. registered)

### Returns & Adjustments
- Return requests count
- Refund and return related reports

## AI Analytics Workspace Features

- **Conversational analytics** inside the admin panel — ask questions in plain English about revenue, orders, products, categories, customers, discounts, carts, returns, and regions.
- **AI-generated summaries** with supporting key numbers, data tables, and charts — charts are only generated when you ask for them.
- **Read-only execution** on approved nopCommerce data — the AI cannot modify store data.
- **Saved prompts panel** for one-click access to recurring questions (e.g. "Revenue last 30 days", "Top selling products", "New vs returning customers").
- **Recent prompt activity** panel showing timestamp and outcome for each question asked.
- **Clarification handling** — if a question is ambiguous, the AI asks a follow-up before running the analysis instead of guessing.

## General Features

- **Quick Date Shortcuts** – One-click "Last 7 days", "Last 30 days", and "Last 90 days" buttons on the dashboard.
- **Data Scope Transparency** – The Configuration screen clearly states that analytics use only live nopCommerce store data (orders, customers, products, carts, discounts, returns, and addresses) — no external marketing or third-party data.
- **Prompt Log Retention** – Configure how many days AI prompt logs are retained.
- **Dedicated Admin Permission** – Restrict access to the plugin's screens to specific admin roles.
- **Localization Support** – All field labels and hints use nopCommerce's locale resource system for easy translation.
- **Professionally developed and fully supported** by Xcellence-IT.
